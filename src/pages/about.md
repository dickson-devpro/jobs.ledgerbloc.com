We publish guides for people looking for work abroad with visa sponsorship — what specific roles pay, which visa route applies, which employers sponsor, and how to apply without being scammed.

Most information on this subject is either a government page written for lawyers or a listing site with no explanation attached. Someone searching for whether a care assistant role in the UK can be sponsored, or what a truck driver earns in Canada under an LMIA, deserves a straight answer with the actual requirements in it.

## What we cover

Guides organised by destination — the USA, Canada, the UK and Ireland, Europe, Australia and New Zealand, and the Gulf — each covering specific roles: pay, visa route, employer requirements, and how to apply.

## How the job links work

Every guide includes a link to a **live search on Indeed**, filtered to roles posted in the last seven days. We do not store or copy job listings. When you click, you see whatever is genuinely open at that moment, and you apply directly with the employer.

## How we produce our content

- **We start from real questions** — what people actually search for, role by role and country by country.
- **We work from primary sources** — government visa guidance, published sponsor registers, and employer listings.
- **We use technology in our workflow, with human oversight.** Research and drafting are assisted by modern tools, and articles are reviewed before publication.
- **We update rather than abandon.** Salary bands, visa rules and sponsor lists change. Guides carry a last-reviewed date.

## What we are not

We are not a recruiter, an employer, an immigration adviser, or a law firm. We do not offer jobs, take applications, process visas, or represent anyone.

Salary figures are indicative and vary by employer, region and experience. They are not offers. Always read each listing's own wording on sponsorship, apply through the employer's official channel, and **never pay anyone who guarantees a job or a visa** — that guarantee does not exist, and charging candidates for it is illegal in most of the countries we cover.

For immigration decisions, consult a licensed professional in the relevant country.
